What is Fitt?

Fitt is a private career companion. You build your record once, paste any job, and Fitt researches the company and the role and tailors a CV and interview prep that fit — with everything traceable to what you logged.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your Vault, analyses and documents stay only on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What do the plans include?

Everyone can export their tailored CV. Free gives 1 analysis a month and 10 free CV PDF exports. Fitt Pro (39 SAR/month or 390 SAR/year) adds 15 analyses a month, unlimited CV exports, and every deliverable — gap analysis, positioning strategy, the interview briefcase, outreach email, ATS text and LinkedIn summary. Fitt Pro Max (109 SAR/month or 1090 SAR/year) uses our most capable engine for the deepest research and best-written results, adds priority processing, and gives 20 analyses a month. Pro and Pro Max are auto-renewable subscriptions; they renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I manage, cancel, or restore my subscription?

Manage or cancel anytime in Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings inside Fitt, tap the Subscription row, then Restore — purchases restore to any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open the Vault → Settings → Delete Vault. Deleting the app also removes all Fitt data from your device.
