Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Fitt is designed to be private by default. Your career record, your analyses and the documents Fitt generates live only on your iPhone. Fitt has no user accounts and no sign-in.

What stays on your device

Everything you add to your Vault — your CV, achievements, projects, metrics, certificates, memberships, interests, references and any attached files or photos — is stored only on your device. Your saved opportunities, match analyses and exported PDFs are also stored only on your device. We do not upload, sync, or back up this content to our servers.

What is processed when you run an analysis

When you tap Analyze, Fitt performs live research to help tailor your application. In that process:

  • The job text you paste and publicly available information about the company and the person who posted the role are sent to our research service so it can gather public web information (news, announcements, public professional profiles).
  • The matching of that role against your Vault happens on your device. Your raw Vault data is not sent during research.
  • To generate documents (such as a tailored CV), only the specific facts your device selects are sent to the service transiently, used to produce the result, and not stored on our servers or in logs.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show third-party advertising and do not run third-party analytics or tracking on your Vault content.
  • We do not collect your name, email, or contacts. Fitt has no account system.

Purchases

Fitt Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we do not receive or store your payment details. Fitt only records, on your device, whether Pro is unlocked.

Third-party services

Our research service uses reputable web-search and AI providers to fetch and summarize public information about the job and company you are analyzing. These providers process only the public/job text described above, not your Vault, and act as processors on our behalf.

Your control & deletion

You can export your Vault as a JSON file, or permanently delete it from within the app (Settings → Delete Vault). Deleting the app removes all Fitt data from your device.

Children

Fitt is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
